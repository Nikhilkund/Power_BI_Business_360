# Power_BI_Business_360

## Overview
**Power_BI_Business_360** is a comprehensive business intelligence solution built in Power BI, offering interactive dashboards across multiple business dimensions: Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive views. These dashboards provide aggregated data over custom time periods, enabling in-depth analysis and strategic decision-making across products, customers, countries, and more.

## Features
### 1. Finance View - P&L Analysis Tool
- Developed a detailed Profit and Loss (P&L) analysis tool.
- Users can filter and aggregate data by customer, product, or country.
- Ability to filter by custom time periods for performance comparisons.
- **Key financial metrics:**
  - Net Sales
  - Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
  - Gross Margin
  - Operating Expenses
  - Net Income

### 2. Sales View - Customer Performance Dashboard
- Interactive dashboard for tracking and analyzing customer performance.
- **Key metrics:**
  - Net Sales: Revenue after discounts.
  - Gross Margin: Profitability insights (Net Sales minus COGS).
- Features include:
  - Identifying top-performing customers.
  - Tracking sales growth trends.
  - Analyzing customer profitability over time.

### 3. Marketing View - Product Performance Analysis Tool
- Product performance analysis tool designed for strategic decision-making.
- **Key metrics:**
  - Net Sales
  - Gross Margin
- Useful for:
  - Marketing campaigns
  - Product launches
  - Product lifecycle management

### 4. Supply Chain View - Supply Chain Dashboard
- Customizable dashboard to monitor supply chain performance.
- **Key metrics:**
  - Forecast Accuracy
  - Net Error: Difference between actual and forecasted demand.
  - Risk Profiles: Customizable by product, segment, category, or customer.
- Provides granular insights into supply chain efficiency and improvement areas.

### 5. Executive View - Executive Dashboard
- Consolidates insights from Finance, Sales, Marketing, and Supply Chain views.
- Designed for high-level strategic decision-making.
- **Visualizations:**
  - KPI Indicators
  - Summary charts and graphs
  - Trend analysis

## Usage

- **Finance View:** Use slicers to filter by customer, product, or country. The interactive visualizations allow for dynamic, time-based comparisons of financial performance.
- **Sales View:** Select KPIs to analyze customer profitability and growth trends over time.
- **Marketing View:** Analyze product performance to assess profitability and adjust marketing strategies.
- **Supply Chain View:** Monitor supply chain efficiency, forecast accuracy, and identify risks in real-time.
- **Executive View:** Utilize top-level summaries and visualizations to get a comprehensive view of business performance for strategic decision-making.

## Key Technologies
- **Power BI:** For creating interactive dashboards and visualizations.
- **SQL/Excel:** Used for data preparation and analysis.
- **Data Sources:** Financial, sales, marketing, and supply chain datasets.

## Future Enhancements
- Integration with real-time data sources for automatic updates.
- Enhanced forecasting models for more accurate demand and sales predictions.
- Adding advanced filters and drill-down capabilities for deeper analysis.
